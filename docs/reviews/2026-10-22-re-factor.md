## Minor
### 🟡 Substring containment in metric column matching for metric delta, `backend/src/services/retrieval/structured_query_service.py:774`
**Problem**: In `_execute_metric_delta`, column resolution checks `col.name.lower() in q_lower` rather than applying word boundary regular expressions.
**Why it matters**: Short column identifiers such as id, tax, rate, or net can match substrings inside conversational words in change queries, potentially selecting an unintended column. In contrast, `_find_target_metric_column` uses word boundary matching `re.search(rf"\b{re.escape(col.name.lower())}\b", q_lower)`.
**Suggested fix**: Standardize column name resolution in `_execute_metric_delta` to use word boundary regular expressions matching the column identifier.
