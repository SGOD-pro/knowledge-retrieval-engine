## Strengths

- **Atomic distributed transactions and lease release**: DynamoDB `commit_manifest` atomically combines publication manifest persistence with conditional lease release in a single `transact_write_items` call. This completely eliminates the race condition where a late worker could clear a newer worker's active lease.
- **Native atomic list append reconciliation**: DynamoDB budget reconciliation leverages native `list_append` guarded by condition expressions rather than memory read modify write patterns, guaranteeing idempotent reconciliations and preventing concurrent worker markers from being overwritten.
- **Strict provenance and identity isolation**: `extract_pdf_relationships` verifies chunk provenance against document, version, and workspace boundaries, preserves chunk `page_number`, and strictly disallows untrusted model-invented disambiguation tokens unless backed by authoritative source mappings.
- **Syntactic clause grounding and modality filtering**: Grounding validation partitions compound sentences into discrete clauses across punctuation and conjunction boundaries, successfully preventing false relationship extraction across disjoint clauses while rejecting conditional, hypothetical, and reported speech claims.
- **Provider concurrency bounding and capacity retention**: `KnowledgeGraphProviderConcurrencyController` bounds active Bedrock calls and queued submissions, retaining capacity slots until underlying provider work completes to prevent caller timeouts from flooding the provider.
- **Exhaustive regression test suite**: The 38-test suite in `test_knowledge_graph_slice2.py` thoroughly tests all counterexamples, CountTokens failure modes, concurrent lease takeovers, stale worker fencing, crash restart recoveries, independent SQLite connections, and live DynamoDB Local conditional transactions.
